# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1][covenant].

## In short

Be kind, assume good faith, give clear feedback. Disagree on technical
points without making it personal. Help newcomers when you can.

## Reporting

Send a private email to `oss@downsized.nl` if you see behaviour that
breaks the covenant. Reports stay confidential by default. The
maintainer acknowledges within seven days, investigates, and follows
up with the reporter on the outcome.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
